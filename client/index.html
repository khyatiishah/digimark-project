Sugarcane Irrigation Advisory: AI Smart Irrigation for Farmers

The Sugarcane AI Irrigation Advisory platform helps sugarcane farmers in India decide exactly when and how much to irrigate. It combines soil moisture sensor data, local weather forecasts and crop-stage models to give a clear, plot-level irrigation schedule, so farmers save water and electricity while protecting cane yield.

Smart irrigation schedules from soil moisture sensors

IoT soil moisture and temperature sensors report root-zone conditions for every plot. The advisory engine compares these readings with the target moisture for the crop stage and recommends the next drip irrigation date, run time and water volume per acre.

Fertigation plans and sugarcane yield prediction

The fertigation module suggests urea, DAP and potash doses by crop age, and the yield predictor estimates tonnes per acre from moisture, NDVI canopy health and plot area. Alerts and printable plot reports keep farmers and field officers informed.
